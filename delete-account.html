Delete your account

Home in Order, operated by SGTechStudio

In the app

  1. Open Home in Order and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile (bottom bar), then My Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

By email

If you can't use the app, email support@homeinorder.org from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm the request with you and delete the account within 30 days.

Before you delete

  • Household admin with other members? Make another member admin first (Profile → Household Settings), or remove the other members.
  • Unsettled expenses? Settle up in Expenses first.
  • Want a copy of your data? Use Profile → About & Support → Export My Data before deleting.

What gets deleted

  • Your login, profile and name.
  • Your notification settings and device tokens.
  • If you're the only member of your household, the entire household and everything in it, including uploaded photos.

What stays

  • If your household has other members, items you added there (such as chores, lists and settled expenses) stay available to them, with your name removed. They're shared household records rather than personal ones.
  • Deleted data may remain in our hosting provider's backups for a short period before it's overwritten.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
