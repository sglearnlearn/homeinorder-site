Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Data controller

The data controller responsible for your personal data is ATHLORY INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED, a company incorporated in India, with its registered office at Shop No. 23, Rushabh Apt, Madhuban Township, Vasai Road East, Vasai, Thane, Maharashtra, India 401202. "We", "us" and "our" refer to that company. Contact: support@homeinorder.org.

This policy covers the Home in Order app (the "app").

1. What we collect

  • Account: your name and email address, and your password (stored only as a secure hash) or, if you use Sign in with Google, your Google account identifier.
  • What your household adds: lists, chores, calendar events, meal plans, expenses and bills (amounts and how costs are split), inventory, checklists, travel plans, wishlist items, habits, care tasks, and memories with photos. Care tasks can include health appointments for a child, relative or pet in your care, entered by you.
  • Device: a push notification token (if you allow notifications), your phone's time zone (so reminders arrive in your morning), and crash reports (error details, app version, device model and operating system, and your account's random ID).
  • Messages: your email address and message if you contact us. Our hosting provider also keeps routine technical logs, such as IP addresses.

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't sell your data.

2. How we use it

Only to provide the app: sharing information within your household, tracking shared costs, sending the reminders and account emails you need, fixing errors, and replying to you. We do this to perform our agreement with you (the Terms of Service) and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

3. Who can see it

  • Your household: what you add is visible to its other members, except habits, which are private to you.
  • Nobody else: other users can't reach your household's data; this is enforced by the database itself.
  • Our service providers, only to run the service: Supabase (accounts, database and photos), Resend (account emails), Expo and Google Firebase (push notifications), Google (Sign in with Google), Sentry (crash reports), and Spaceship and Gmail (our support email).

We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

4. Where it's stored

Your data is stored in the EU (Ireland), and crash reports in the EU (Germany). Some providers are in the United States, so limited data, such as your email address for account emails or a reminder's text, is processed there under the providers' contractual safeguards.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists, and you can edit or delete items at any time. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → My Profile → Delete Account) or by email. Items you added to a household with other members stay available to them, with your name removed; a household with no other members is deleted entirely, including photos. Deleted data may remain in backups for a short period. Details: Delete your account.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you can access your data (in the app: Profile → About & Support → Export My Data), correct it, delete it, object to or restrict certain processing, and complain to us or to your data protection authority. Email support@homeinorder.org; we aim to reply within 30 days.

7. Children

You must be at least 16 to use the app. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if you believe a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

8. Changes

If we make significant changes, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app or by email first.
